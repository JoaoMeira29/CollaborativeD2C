What happens when you sign in

  1. You enter your email and password

    Only the main platform asks for them. No other service ever sees your password.

  2. You get a session that lasts one hour

    The platform signs it, so it cannot be altered without being noticed.

  3. Each service checks that session

    While it is valid you move between services without signing in again.
